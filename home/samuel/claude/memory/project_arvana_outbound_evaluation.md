# Arvana evaluation for datatactics outbound

User met with Arvana (https://www.arvana.io/) on 2026-05-20 to discuss outbound lead generation for datatactics.de.

## Arvana pitch
Signal-based outbound: pull from business databases, enrich with news/events about each company, filter to ICP, then send personalized email + LinkedIn messages tying datatactics value prop to detected pain points.

## Pricing (per month, as quoted 2026-05-20)
- LinkedIn-only campaign: €2,000/mo
- LinkedIn + email combined: €3,000/mo

## Reputability read
Legitimate company, not a scam. Registered Berlin GmbH (Stresemannstrasse 23), founded 2021, 11–50 employees per Sortlist/LinkedIn (their own site inflates to "30+ employees, 200+ clients, 5+ years"). Real named clients: ProAlpha, Trovarit, SmapOne. ~10 positive Sortlist reviews. No Trustpilot/ProvenExpert presence. Most named clients are SaaS, not consulting — sales-cycle fit for a DE data/analytics consultancy is unproven.

## Risks to push on before signing
- §7 UWG / GDPR exposure on the cold email side — DE has narrow "mutmaßliches Interesse" interpretation. Who carries liability?
- Case studies for *consulting firms* their size, not just SaaS
- Pilot length: push for 3 months, not 6–12. Outbound needs ~6–8 weeks to ramp
- Define "qualified meeting" contractually (ICP fit, role, company size, no-show handling)
- References — two current clients, datatactics' choice
- Internal capacity: who at datatactics handles replies within 24h?

## Build-vs-buy analysis (for datatactics)
Could be built in-house: ~8–12 weeks initial (1 eng) or 4–6 weeks (2 eng). Ongoing ~0.5 FTE + €500–1,500/mo tooling. Year-1 DIY ≈ €40–75k; **not** cheaper than Arvana's €36k.

**Smart-shop hybrid:** build data + signal + LLM personalization in-house (the IP), buy delivery (Smartlead/Instantly + Heyreach). Cuts build by half, avoids deliverability cat-and-mouse.

**Strategic upside for datatactics specifically:** as a data company, this is a potential productizable asset and a credible public case study, not just an internal cost center.

## Claude's recommendation
3-month Arvana pilot (LinkedIn-only first, €6k total) in parallel with a 4–6 week internal spike on the data/signal/personalization layer. After 3 months: real ICP data + a foundation to take in-house if it works.

How to apply: if user revisits this decision, the 3-month-pilot-plus-internal-spike is the recommendation. If they ask about other DE outbound agencies, contrast against Arvana's signal-based pitch and 11–50-person scale.
